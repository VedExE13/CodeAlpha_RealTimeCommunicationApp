# Brag Plan: Open Room

## What is this app?
Open Room is a private team space for encrypted messages, files, shared sketches, and optional peer-to-peer calls.

## The angle
Make this feel like stepping into a considered room after dark: conversation, shared whiteboard, and a call stage where media stays visibly off until the user chooses.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
“A room for the work you share.” reveals a room with two fictional participants and explicit OFF controls.

## Key moments (the middle)
- Sample chat explicitly says device-side encryption.
- A whiteboard mockup uses the project’s pen, color, shape, and eraser concepts.
- The call panel shows microphone, camera, and screen share off.

## Outro / punchline
“Private by design.”

## User flow worth showing
Open a room → exchange a message and sketch together → choose whether to enable call media.

## Tone
- Preset: cinematic
- Creative direction: A quiet midnight room: electric indigo wayfinding, open space, and deliberate controls.
- Interpretation: Spacious scenes, controlled motion, and a clear privacy posture rather than spectacle.

## Format: landscape — 1920x1080
## Duration: 20 seconds

## Visual identity (from the project)
- Background: ink indigo `#111522`
- Accent: electric periwinkle `#98a9ff`
- Text: warm white `#f4f3f0`
- Display font: DM Sans in product; Arial fallback in film.
- Body font: DM Sans in product; Arial fallback in film.
- Strongest visual element: room surface with separate chat, whiteboard, and call controls.

## Share copy (draft)
Open Room brings messages, shared sketches, and optional calls into one private space. The sample call keeps camera and microphone off until someone enables them.

## Audio direction
- Role: restrained cinematic support.
- Music: Happy Beats / Business Moves Vol. 1, Sascha Ende (CC BY 4.0), mixed quietly.
- Music treatment: low bed; soften under message and whiteboard scenes, trim at the close.
- Music cue guidance: bundled cue preset; whiteboard reveal resolves near 17.02s strong cue. Sequential message details follow the half-second grid.
- Audio-reactive treatment: none; extraction support unavailable in this local setup.
- SFX posture: sparse; restrained message and board contact sounds only.
- Audio-coupled moments: room reveal; chat arrival; whiteboard reveal; final room mark.
- Restraint rule: no real names, private keys, message history, or active media. All participant content is fictional and staged.

## Storyboard
### Scene 1 — Enter the room — 3s
The room header, two fictional participants, and explicit OFF controls. Sequential/interaction: room structure assembles in layers. Audio intent: quiet reveal. Audio-coupled idea: room title lands on a cue.
### Scene 2 — Words — 5s
Two clearly fictional sample messages; encryption-on-device copy from the project. Sequential/interaction: messages arrive in order. Audio intent: soft, conversational. Audio-coupled idea: messages stagger to the beat grid.
### Scene 3 — Ideas — 4.5s
A drawn whiteboard and recognizable toolbar concepts. Sequential/interaction: pen strokes draw onto the board. Audio intent: subtle tactile mark. Audio-coupled idea: final stroke resolves on the cue.
### Scene 4 — Optional calls — 4.5s
Fictional participants in a call stage. MIC OFF, CAMERA OFF, and SHARE SCREEN are visible; no real capture. Sequential/interaction: controls settle individually. Audio intent: restrained. Audio-coupled idea: controls arrive naturally, without implying transmission.
### Scene 5 — Close — 3s
Open Room and “Private by design.” Sequential/interaction: none. Intentional reading hold is annotated in the composition. Audio intent: quiet resolve. Audio-coupled idea: music fades under the room mark.
